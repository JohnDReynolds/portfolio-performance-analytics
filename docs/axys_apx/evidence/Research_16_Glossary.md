## Preserved Conclusions

- Product, platform, integration, reporting, and domain definitions must retain
  the confidence and product-specific boundaries established by their subject
  chapters.
- Exact field names, transaction codes, report names, and file layouts belong
  in Chapter 15 or the owning subject chapter, not in the glossary.
- The former deep-IMEX addendum described Custodial Integrator artifacts and an
  internal `imex_catalog` design. Those terms and limitations are maintained in
  Research 12 and Chapter 12.
- Definitions based only on public practitioner material must not be promoted
  into official native Axys/APX specifications.

## Maintenance Rule

Do not add independent product research here. Add evidence to the owning
subject research file and update the glossary only when a concise definition or
ambiguity note improves navigation. Git history retains the earlier glossary
research draft and source inventory.
